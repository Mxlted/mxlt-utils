# 0.3.7 - Apps launcher fix

Fixes mxlt-utils missing from Apple's Apps launcher after installation.

The app now declares its macOS platform explicitly, preventing macOS from incorrectly identifying the Apple silicon build as an iOS app. It continues to run in the menu bar without a Dock icon.

## Download

**[Download for Mac](https://github.com/Mxlted/mxlt-utils/releases/download/v0.3.7/mxlt-utils-0.3.7-macos-arm64.dmg)** · [SHA256 checksums](https://github.com/Mxlted/mxlt-utils/releases/download/v0.3.7/SHA256SUMS.txt)

Requires **macOS 14 or later on Apple silicon**.

Quit the running copy of mxlt-utils, open the DMG, and drag **mxlt-utils** into **Applications** to replace it. Launch the updated app. Your settings are retained.

The app and DMG are Developer ID signed, notarized, and stapled.
